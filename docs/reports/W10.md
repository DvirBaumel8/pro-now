Status: **DONE 2026-09-30.** Dvir, with Claude Code. Scope:

## What was built

- **Headers** (`plugins/security.ts`, helmet): a CSP with no inline script
  and connections only to this origin, its socket, storage and Sentry;
  HSTS and `upgrade-insecure-requests` in production over https;
  `frame-ancestors 'none'`; referrer policy.
- **Input:** zod on every body and query, proven by a sweep of every
  `/api/*` route with garbage as three roles (no 500 anywhere; it found
  one, now a 400). A 64 KB body limit.
- **Logs without personal data:** cookies and authorization redacted,
  query strings dropped, every line scrubbed of emails, phones and tokens.
- **Dependencies:** `npm run audit:shipped` in CI, over the 248 packages
  that actually ship. nodemailer 7 → 10 fixed the one high advisory.
- **Health:** `/api/health` (liveness) and `/api/ready` (database, PostGIS,
  storage; 503 without error text).
- **Neon runbook and a tested restore** (`docs/16`): pooled URL for the
  app, `DIRECT_DATABASE_URL` for migrations; `npm run drill:backup`.
- **Client address:** one resolution (`TRUST_PROXY_HOPS`) for every
  per-person limit, Better Auth's included.
- **The Prisma-path accept race** the plan carried over from W1.
- **CI:** the e2e job now runs twice, development and production build.
