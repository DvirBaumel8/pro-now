## What was built

- **One matcher for web and server.** `packages/types/src/request-match.ts`
  (moved from `packages/ui`, which re-exports it). `matchRequest()` returns
  candidates, a **confidence band** (high / medium / low / none) and, when
  needed, **one clarifying question**. Bands, not percentages: a keyword
  count has no calibrated probability behind it.
- **Matcher rules, each driven by a failing sentence:**
  - Negation: "לא צריך/רוצה/מחפש X" and a bare "לא" before a trade's title,
    within the clause. Symptoms ("לא מקרר") are untouched.
  - Context: a car in the sentence gives the car the words it shares with
    the home (door, key, lock, battery), and without a car a car service
    may not claim them. Cues match whole words ("מכונה" is not "מכונית").
  - Ambiguous words (עכבר, מסך, סוללה) resolve by their neighbours, or ask.
  - A room word alone is half the evidence of a problem word ("נזילה במטבח").
  - A match resting only on a near-miss spelling asks instead of suggesting.
- **Golden set with a CI baseline**:
  - Data: `packages/types/test/fixtures/match-golden.he.json` (166 sentences).
    Each row is labelled by source: `real` (21), `plan` (3), `dev-seed` (142).
  - CI fails if top-1, top-2 or "quiet" drops, or if "confidently wrong"
    rises.
- **Server**:
  - `RequestClassifier` interface, with the keyword adapter.
  - A classifier chain that cuts every answer down to catalogue services.
  - `POST /api/v1/match` (signed in; the text is never logged).
  - `POST /api/v1/match/feedback`, storing into the new `match_feedback`
    table (migration `20260930_match_feedback`). Rows are swept after 4
    days (D3) and erased with the account.
- **Web home screen**:
  - Suggestions follow the band: one answer; two answers; or the question
    with equal-weight answers.
  - Every pick after typing sends feedback: what was suggested and what was
    chosen.

## Not done, and why

- **Voice to text (Web Speech API, `he-IL`)**:
  - Not built. It would run speech recognition on the microphone while the
    W4 recorder holds it.
  - If iOS Safari does not share the microphone, that breaks voice notes,
    which work today, and nothing here can test it. The plan itself
    requires a real-iPhone check.
  - Next step: a 15-minute check on an iPhone (recognition alone, then
    alongside recording), then build what that shows is possible.
- **The model-backed classifier**: an interface and a place in the chain
  only. Vendor and model are decision D2. `REQUEST_CLASSIFIERS` accepts
  only `keyword`.
- **The admin review of `match_feedback`** is W8. **The professional's
  "wrong service" flag** is W7 (the column `proFlaggedWrong` exists).
- **Web `understand` hook**: not wired. With only the keyword classifier,
  the server would return exactly what the browser already computed.
  Wire it when a second classifier exists.
