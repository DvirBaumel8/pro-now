## What was built

- **No money in the app (D1)**, as a server mode (`IN_APP_PAYMENTS=off`,
  the default):
  - A quote is approved by the system when sent.
  - Confirming completion moves COMPLETED → REVIEW_PENDING and records a
    `SETTLED_OUTSIDE_APP` receipt, computed by the same settlement code
    as the ledger path.
  - No payment or ledger rows are written.
  - `sandbox` keeps the full ledger path for developing payments.
- **Live job events:**
  - Every `job_events` row, whoever writes it, is published through a
    Prisma query extension.
  - The job socket sends `READY` on connect, then `JOB_EVENT` per event.
  - Clients re-read the job over REST.
- **The web job screen** (`/jobs/:id`), built from the product's shared
  screens:
  - searching (real supply only, with a cancel);
  - the match card, with the professional's own price;
  - tracking through every stage;
  - completion confirmed by the customer;
  - the review with a receipt of what is owed directly;
  - closed.

  "Nobody available" and "cancelled" say so. Home leads to it after
  sending and through a capsule.
- **Ordering for someone else:**
  - The person at home (name and Israeli mobile) belongs to this order only.
  - A 4-digit door code is issued at assignment, inside the atomic accept.
  - The link:
    - The orderer shares it from the job screen (share sheet or clipboard).
    - Only its sha256 is stored.
    - It expires in 24 h and rotates.
  - A public page `/s/:token` shows who is coming, what was checked, when,
    and the code, with no address and no price.
- **The copy the customer reads** was brought in line with D1 (QA #4, #6,
  #8, #11): the product's shared screens had been written for in-app
  payment.
