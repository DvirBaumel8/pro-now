# W8 — Admin: epic report

Status: **DONE 2026-09-30.** Dvir, with Claude Code. Scope:
`docs/21-PRODUCTION-PLAN.md` W8. The admin lives inside `apps/web`, as
D7 proposed; the Next.js scaffold in `apps/admin` is not used.

## What was built

- **Applications**, one decision at a time, each audited:
  - the account;
  - each licence, with its expiry;
  - each service.
  - Documents open through 2-minute links. A refusal needs a reason.
  - A service can be approved only after the account, and only when its
    licences pass dispatch's own rule.
- **The job inspector:** jobs by status, and each job's story from
  `job_events`, offers, quotes and the review.
- **Users and roles:**
  - Search by email.
  - CUSTOMER and PROFESSIONAL are granted or removed with a reason.
  - **ADMIN is not grantable**: it comes only from the `ADMIN_EMAILS`
    allowlist.
- **Market switches** per service, each change with a reason: visible to
  customers, open to professionals, dispatched. **Dispatch now honours the
  last one** (it was hard-coded).
- **Match feedback** (W5): the rows where the customer chose something
  other than the first suggestion are marked. Those are the sentences
  for the golden set.
- **Usage:** users, professionals and jobs by status, storage and
  database size, against limits only when configured
  (`STORAGE_LIMIT_BYTES`, `DATABASE_LIMIT_BYTES`).
- **The retention sweep** no longer deletes professionals' evidence (QA #1).
