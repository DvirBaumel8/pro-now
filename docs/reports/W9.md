## What was built

- **The person's own channel** (`WS /v1/ws/me`): offers reach the
  professional the moment dispatch sends them. The 3 s poll is now a
  15 s fallback. Notifications reach anyone signed in. The job channel
  also says when the professional's position changed. As before, the
  socket carries the fact and REST the state.
- **One dispatcher on the job event bus, one policy table**
  (`domain/notifications/policy.ts`):

  | Event | Who | Channels |
  |---|---|---|
  | Offer sent | the professional | socket + push |
  | Accepted, on the way, arrived, quote | the customer | in-app + push |
  | Finished, or nobody available | the customer | in-app + push + email |
  | Cancelled by the customer | the professional | in-app + push |

  Messages to the professional use the gender they chose.
- **Channels:**
  - **In-app:** an inbox with read and unread.
  - **Email:** through an outbox and a retrying worker (backoff; gives up
    after 6 and keeps the record).
  - **Web Push:** with our own VAPID keys; a subscription the push service
    reports gone is deleted. The service worker shows the notification
    right-to-left and opens the screen it points at.
  - **SMS:** an interface only (D4).
- **Live ETA:** recomputed from the professional's latest position while
  they are on the way, by the same maps provider dispatch uses.
- **Erasure:** account deletion also removes the inbox, push subscriptions
  and unsent mail.
