## 5. Deliberately not copied

- **"3 פנויים", "3 פנויים עכשיו", "מצאנו 3 התאמות זמינות"**: the demo's
  invented supply. The product says "נבדוק זמינות כששולחים" and shows
  matches only when the server has them (CLAUDE.md §3).
- **290 ₪ on the form against "החל מ־285" on the page**: the demo rounds
  one and not the other. The product shows 285 in both (#32).
- **"מאושר בכרטיס"**: the demo still says the amount is approved on the
  card. Under D1 the product says the customer pays the professional
  directly (#43).
