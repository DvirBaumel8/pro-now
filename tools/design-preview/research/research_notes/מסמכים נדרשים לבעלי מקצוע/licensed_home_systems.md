# Licensing requirements in Israel (as of Sept 2026) for self-employed home-systems trades: electrician, alarm/CCTV, gas, plumbing, AC, solar water heaters, appliance repair, locksmith

Research date: 2026-09-29. Method: primary sources wherever reachable. These are gov.il pages read in a real browser, since gov.il blocks plain fetchers with Cloudflare; the Ministry of Labor and Ministry of Energy live lookup tools, which I opened and exercised myself; and legal texts on Nevo and Wikisource, read through a summarising fetcher. Most gov.il quotes are verbatim page text. The Nevo and Wikisource section contents came through a summariser and may be paraphrased, so check them against the source before legal use. This is not legal advice. Anything marked "Inference" is my reading, not a sourced fact.

## Q1. Electrician (home faults, sockets, lighting): what is legally required, which class, and what is illegal without it

### Takeaway
Any "electrical work" needs a personal licence from the Ministry of Labor's electrician-licensing unit. Electrical work means installing, testing, changing, repairing or dismantling an electrical installation, including supervising such work. For typical home jobs (single-phase up to 1x40A, three-phase up to 3x80A), the working classes are חשמלאי-מעשי (practical electrician) and above. A three-phase home over 40A needs at least חשמלאי-מוסמך (certified electrician). A חשמלאי-עוזר (assistant electrician) may only help under supervision. Licences are verifiable free, without a captcha, on a public Ministry of Labor query.

### Cited Findings
- **Statutory prohibition, Electricity Law 1954 §6(א):** "לא יעסוק אדם בביצוע עבודת חשמל, אלא אם יש בידו רישיון מאת המנהל" (no one may carry out electrical work without a licence from the director). "עבודת חשמל" is defined as "התקנה, בדיקה, שינוי, תיקון או פירוק של מתקן חשמלי, לרבות השגחה על ביצוע עבודה" (installing, testing, changing, repairing or dismantling an electrical installation, including supervision). "מתקן חשמלי" (electrical installation) covers any installation used to produce, carry, distribute, consume, store or transform electricity — [חוק החשמל (Wikisource)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/%D7%97%D7%95%D7%A7_%D7%94%D7%97%D7%A9%D7%9E%D7%9C); [חוק החשמל (Nevo)](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law01/999_468.htm)
- **Penalty, §11:** "מאסר שנה אחת או קנס אלף לירות או שני הענשים כאחד" (one year's imprisonment or a fine of 1,000 lira, or both). This is the wording in the text as published. The fine is in pre-1980 currency and is converted under general penal law — [Nevo](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law01/999_468.htm). Section 6(ג) lets the director cancel or restrict a licence if its use endangers people or property, or after a conviction for an offence connected with electrical work — [gov.il limited-license notice](https://www.gov.il/he/pages/limited-license)
- **Classes under תקנות החשמל (רשיונות), תשמ"ה-1985 (the licensing regulations):** — [Nevo 159_015](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law01/159_015.htm)
  - חשמלאי-עוזר (assistant, §9): may only help carry out low-voltage electrical work, under supervision.
  - חשמלאי-מעשי (practical, §11): low voltage, installations up to 1x40A. Above 1x40A only under supervision.
  - חשמלאי-מוסמך (certified, §13): low voltage up to 3x80A.
  - חשמלאי-ראשי (chief, §15): low voltage up to 3x250A, including drawing up plans.
  - חשמלאי-טכנאי (technician, §17): up to 3x400A. חשמלאי-הנדסאי (practical engineer, §19): up to 3x630A. חשמלאי-מהנדס (engineer, §21): any work.
  - חשמלאי-בודק (inspector, §§22–27), types 1 to 3: installation inspections.
  - חשמלאי-מסוייג (restricted, §28): a specific type of work only, often limited to one workplace.
  - חשמלאי-שירות (service, §29): 12 fields of specialisation. These include household electrical appliances (washing machines, refrigerators, dishwashers, ovens, dryers, heaters and so on), cooling and air-conditioning systems, antennas, instrumentation and control, electronic instrumentation, radio/audio/TV/video, generators and lifts.
  - Low voltage means ≤1000V; high voltage means >1000V.
- **Issuing authority:** the electrician-licensing unit (היחידה לרישוי חשמלאים), part of מינהל אסדרת עיסוקים (the Occupational Regulation Administration) in the Ministry of Labor. It is the only body that grants electrical-work licences — [המחלקה לרישוי חשמלאים](https://www.gov.il/he/departments/units/electricity-licensing-unit); [landing page](https://www.gov.il/he/departments/topics/electricians-licensure/govil-landing-page)
- **Licences are digital and renew yearly.** Since March 2022 licences are issued and renewed only digitally — [digital licences notice](https://www.gov.il/he/pages/digital-electric). Renewal: licences normally expire on 30 April. Licences expiring on 30.4.2026 were automatically extended by three months, to 31.7.2026, because of the security situation — [extension notice](https://www.gov.il/he/pages/electric-license-extend); [renewal 2026](https://www.gov.il/he/pages/license-renew2025)
- **Water heaters (דוודים):** the Ministry of Labor states that installing or maintaining a water heater requires at least a חשמלאי-מעשי licence, and that חשמלאי-שירות and חשמלאי-מסויג do not qualify. This applies if the job involves isolating the supply, checking the feed cables are dead, disconnecting or connecting the feed wires to the thermostat and heating element (including earthing), or checking consumption — [gov.il boiler notice, updated 16.06.2026](https://www.gov.il/he/pages/boiler-installation-011221)
- **What does not count as electrical work:** changing a bulb, or changing a screw-in or replaceable-element fuse, needs no tool and is not treated as "electrical work". The sources are industry pages interpreting the regulations, not primary text — [hashmalnet](https://www.hashmalnet.co.il/%D7%90%D7%99%D7%96%D7%94-%D7%A2%D7%91%D7%95%D7%93%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%97%D7%A9%D7%9E%D7%9C-%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%AA%D7%A8-%D7%9C%D7%91%D7%A6%D7%A2-%D7%9C%D7%9C%D7%90-%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%A9%D7%99%D7%95%D7%9F-%D7%97%D7%A9/)

### How to verify (electrician)
- **Public registry:** "שאילתה לעוסקים בעבודות חשמל" (query for people licensed to do electrical work) — https://data.labor.gov.il/Electricians/GetLicenseList.aspx, linked from the [Ministry landing page](https://www.gov.il/he/departments/topics/electricians-licensure/govil-landing-page). I checked it live on 2026-09-29.
  - **Search by any one of:** licence number, ID number, passport number, or first name plus surname.
  - **Result fields:** name, licence number, licence type (e.g. חשמלאי-ראשי, חשמלאי-עוזר), permissions and restrictions (free text, e.g. "רשאי לבצע עבודות חשמל ולתכנן מתקנים במתח נמוך עד 3X250 אמפר"), licence issue date, and "valid until".
  - **Coverage:** the page states it lists only electricians whose licence is valid and paid, and **excludes the חשמלאי-מסויג type**. It does not show applications still in process. The unit's own list is the binding one; the site is a convenience.
  - **Access:** free and without a captcha. It is a classic ASP.NET WebForms postback page (ViewState, text fields, image submit button), so it could be automated by form post. I found no official API. Automating it is technically possible, but no terms-of-use permission was found.
- **QR code on the licence:** the new digital licences carry a QR code that can be scanned to confirm validity. The Ministry warns that some electricians whose licence was cancelled or restricted keep working with a licence that only looks valid. It instructs anyone employing an electrician to check validity on the Ministry site as well as seeing the original licence — [limited-license notice](https://www.gov.il/he/pages/limited-license)

### Inferences
- A marketplace job for sockets, lighting or fault-finding in a typical apartment (1x40A) needs, as a minimum eligibility gate, licence type ∈ {מעשי, מוסמך, ראשי, טכנאי, הנדסאי, מהנדס}. For three-phase homes above 40A, require ≥ מוסמך. Exclude עוזר (assistant only), בודק (inspector only), and שירות and מסויג, which are field-limited and explicitly excluded even for water heaters.
- The lookup's "permissions and restrictions" text should be stored and matched against the service's amperage and scope, not just "has a licence".
- Validity must be re-checked at least after every 30 April renewal cycle, allowing for ad-hoc extensions like 2026's.

### Gaps
- I did not find a provision in the Electricity Law itself that makes it an offence to *order* or *employ* an unlicensed electrician. The summarised text shows only the performer's offence. תקנות הבטיחות בעבודה (חשמל) (the 1990 electrical work-safety regulations) may contain employer duties, but I did not verify this.
- The current shekel value of the §11 fine after penal-law conversion was not verified.

## Q2. Alarm and CCTV installers: electrician licence or something else?

### Takeaway
There is **no separate "alarm/CCTV installer" licence** in the sources I found. The Electricity Law has no explicit exemption for extra-low-voltage work, so any connection to 230V mains (power supplies, new socket or spur, panel feed) is electrical work needing an electrician licence. Whether low-voltage signal cabling alone (12V/PoE cameras, detectors) needs a licence is legally unclear and treated permissively in practice. This is flagged as uncertain.

### Cited Findings
- The definitions of "עבודת חשמל" and "מתקן חשמלי" contain no explicit exemption for extra-low voltage — [חוק החשמל (Wikisource)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/%D7%97%D7%95%D7%A7_%D7%94%D7%97%D7%A9%D7%9E%D7%9C)
- An engineering blog interpreting the rules says extra-low voltage is defined as "safety voltage", so anyone can do such work. It also says a technician working on a system fed from 230V needs an electrical licence, and notes that §6 prohibits electrical work without a licence. This is a secondary, interpretive source — [סגל אריאל – מתח נמוך מאוד](https://arielsegal.co.il/topic/%D7%9E%D7%AA%D7%97-%D7%A0%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%9A-%D7%9E%D7%90%D7%95%D7%93/)
- Regulation 29 service-electrician fields include "מכשור ובקרה" (instrumentation and control), "מכשור אלקטרוני" (electronic instrumentation), video/recording and antennas. These are the closest licensed fields to security-system electronics — [Nevo 159_015](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law01/159_015.htm)
- צו רישוי עסקים (עסקים טעוני רישוי), תשע"ג-2013, the order listing which businesses need a municipal business licence, has no item for alarm or security-system installation — [Wikisource](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/%D7%A6%D7%95_%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%A9%D7%95%D7%99_%D7%A2%D7%A1%D7%A7%D7%99%D7%9D_(%D7%A2%D7%A1%D7%A7%D7%99%D7%9D_%D7%98%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%A0%D7%99_%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%A9%D7%95%D7%99)) (checked through a summariser)

### Inferences
- The safe platform rule: for any alarm or CCTV job that includes a mains connection, require an electrician licence (≥ מעשי, or a relevant חשמלאי-שירות field if its scope covers it). For pure low-voltage-only jobs, treat an electrician licence as customary rather than legally required, and record the choice as a product decision.
- Alarm monitoring or guarding services, as distinct from installation, may fall under the private-security legislation. Not researched.

### Gaps
- I found no primary ruling or Ministry guidance that says clearly whether ELV-only alarm/CCTV cabling is "electrical work".
- Whether חוק חוקרים פרטיים ושירותי שמירה (the private investigators and guarding services law) applies to alarm installers was not researched.
- Standard ת"י 1337 (intrusion alarms) and any insurer-mandated installer certification were not verified.

## Q3. Gas technician (LPG / cooking gas, גפ"מ)

### Takeaway
Doing any LPG work without a Ministry of Energy licence is illegal. For home work (the apartment gas installation, connecting or disconnecting a cooker, maintaining cooktops), the relevant class is **מתקין גפ"מ רמה 1** (LPG installer level 1); level 2 covers everything level 1 does and more. The Ministry runs a free public lookup by licence number, ID, or first plus last name. It shows name, licence number, type, expiry, "authorised" status and the full list of permitted work. Holders must carry the licence and show it to the customer on request.

### Cited Findings
- **Legal basis:** חוק הגז הפחמימני המעובה, התשפ"א-2020 (the 2020 LPG law) and תקנות הגז (בטיחות ורישוי) (רישוי העוסקים בעבודות גפ"מ), תשס"ו-2006 (the 2006 regulations licensing LPG workers). The Ministry says LPG work without a licence "is an offence" — [gov.il רישוי עוסקים בגפ"מ, updated 01.01.2026](https://www.gov.il/he/pages/lpg_license)
- **Issuing authority:** מנהל ענייני בטיחות הגז (the gas safety director), in the fuel and gas administration of the Ministry of Energy and Infrastructure. Applications and renewals are online only — [gov.il](https://www.gov.il/he/pages/lpg_license)
- **Regulation 2:** no one may carry out LPG work without a licence. **Licence types (reg. 4):** מתקין גפ"מ רמה 1 (installer level 1), מתקין גפ"מ רמה 2 (installer level 2), מתכנן גפ"מ (designer), מתכנן גפ"מ בכיר (senior designer), and מתכנן מיתקן גפ"מ מיוחד מסוים (designer of a specific special installation) — [Nevo 999_620](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law01/999_620.htm)
- **Level 1 scope, text shown in the official lookup result:**
  - (1) planning, installing, inspecting and maintaining an LPG installation for a single dwelling, up to the tap on the central system;
  - (2) installing a central system in a single residential building, on an approved plan;
  - (3) other uses up to 1,000 kg on an approved plan;
  - (4) maintenance under the supplier's procedure;
  - (5) "חיבור של מכשיר צורך גפ"מ לשימוש ביתי למערכת גפ"מ ביתית וניתוקו… תחזוקת כיריים" (connecting a household gas appliance to a home gas system, disconnecting it, and maintaining cooktops);
  - (6) non-household appliances, if authorised by a supplier.
  - Source: [Ministry of Energy lookup](https://migdal-webpages.energy-apps.org/lpgWorker), checked live 2026-09-29.
- Level 2 covers all level 1 work plus larger installations: self-consumption up to 10 t, larger or special installations on plan, auxiliary storage — [Nevo 999_620](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law01/999_620.htm)
- **Obligations:** a refresher course of at least 8 hours every two years (reg. 11). Renewal is filed ≥60 days before expiry with proof of insurance and of actual work (reg. 7). Reg. 11b: no LPG work unless the worker carries a valid licence, and it must be shown to the service recipient on request — [Nevo 999_620](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law01/999_620.htm) (summarised)

### How to verify (gas)
- **Lookup:** "איתור בעלי רישיון לעסוק בעבודות גפ"מ" (find people licensed for LPG work) — https://migdal-webpages.energy-apps.org/lpgWorker, linked from [gov.il](https://www.gov.il/he/pages/lpg_license). I checked it live.
  - **Input:** licence number, ID number including check digit, or first name plus surname "as on the ID card". The form says one of these is required: licence number, a 9-digit ID, or first plus last name.
  - **Output:** name, licence number, licence type, licence expiry date, status "מורשה" (authorised), and the full list of permitted work.
  - **Coverage:** valid licences only, with no pending applications. The binding list is held by the licensing unit.
  - **Access:** free and no captcha observed. It is an Angular single-page app calling a JSON endpoint (`https://migdal-api.energy-apps.org/api/SearchLpgWorkerForWeb`, seen in the page's network activity). That makes automation technically simple. The endpoint is undocumented and there is no stated licence to reuse it.

### Inferences
- The platform gate for home gas jobs is type ∈ {מתקין גפ"מ רמה 1, רמה 2} and expiry > job date. Keep the licence number and ID on file, and re-query before each expiry.
- Level 1 lists household appliance connection, but the wording is "connecting a household LPG appliance… to a household LPG system". Whether plain cooker installation by an appliance retailer's technician without an LPG licence is lawful is answered no by this text.

### Gaps
- Natural-gas (piped) installer licensing, as distinct from LPG, was not researched.
- The penalty amounts under the 2020 LPG law were not extracted.

## Q4. Plumbing (drain unblocking, leaks, taps)

### Takeaway
**Israel has no licensing law for plumbers** (שרברב/אינסטלטור). The Ministry of Labor certificate "שרברבות תברואתית סוג 1+2" (sanitary plumbing types 1+2) is voluntary. Registration with רשם הקבלנים (the contractors registrar) is required only above monetary thresholds per job: currently more than ₪103,250 in a main branch, or more than ₪54,000 in a sub-branch. Ordinary home repairs are far below both. Plumbing jobs that touch gas or electric heating elements trigger those trades' licences.

### Cited Findings
- "בישראל לא קיים חוק רישוי ייעודי לעיסוק בשרברבות" (Israel has no dedicated licensing law for plumbing). The Ministry of Labor certificate is optional. Contractor branch 190, "מיתקני תברואה (אינסטלציה ומערכות כיבוי אש)" (sanitary installations — plumbing and fire-suppression systems), applies only to larger projects. ת"י 1205 (the plumbing standard) and the sanitary regulations bind the permit applicant and the designer. This is a training-college source, not primary — [מכללת אפיק](https://afikonline.co.il/instalatzia/plumber-license-israel)
- **Contractors Registration Law 1969:** building-engineering work above the monetary scope, or of the professional type, set in the regulations must be done by a registered contractor. "קבלן המבצע עבודות בענף ראשי בהיקף כספי של יותר מ-103,250 ₪ לעבודה או… בענף משנה… יותר מ-54,000 ₪ חייבים להיות רשומים" (a contractor doing more than ₪103,250 per job in a main branch, or more than ₪54,000 in a sub-branch, must register) — [gov.il המדריך לרישום בפנקס הקבלנים, updated 24.02.2026](https://www.gov.il/he/pages/general_information_for_registration_of_contractors)
- **Classification framework:** תקנות רישום קבלנים לעבודות הנדסה בנאיות (סיווג קבלנים רשומים), תשמ"ח-1988, the regulations classifying registered contractors by branch and financial scope — [Nevo 74263](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law00/74263.htm)
- **Contractors registry (פנקס הקבלנים):**
  - Public search by contractor number, name, town, branch, and now also by classification. The printable certificate shows branches, classifications, the qualified staff employed, first-registration date, last-classification date, and a QR code to live data.
  - Status values: "מוקפא" (frozen, at the contractor's request, may not work), and "מוחרג"/"מותלה" (excluded/suspended, may work only on specific projects listed in the registrar's decisions file).
  - Source: [gov.il פנקס הקבלנים, updated 24.09.2026](https://www.gov.il/he/pages/construction_registry)

### Inferences
- For drain unblocking, leaks and taps there is nothing legally required to verify. Trust signals are business registration (עוסק), insurance and, optionally, the Ministry of Labor plumbing certificate.
- Contractor registration becomes relevant only if the platform ever allows large renovation jobs above ₪54,000 or ₪103,250.

### Gaps
- Whether branch 190 counts as a main or a sub-branch for the threshold was not confirmed from primary text.
- Whether the contractors registry has an official open-data or API feed (possibly on data.gov.il) was not verified. The direct search-app URL was not captured; the entry point is the gov.il page above.
- Whether local water corporations (תאגידי מים) require a certified plumber for work at the meter or connection point was not researched.

## Q5. Air-conditioner technician: new mandatory licence (the 2025 AC law)

### Takeaway
**This changed recently.** חוק הסדרת העיסוק בעבודה במערכת קירור או מיזוג אוויר, התשפ"ה-2025 (the law regulating work on cooling and air-conditioning systems) came into force on **15 July 2025**. It makes installing or maintaining cooling/AC systems a licensed occupation, licensed by the Ministry of Labor in three grades. Home split units are grade 1 (up to 18 kW). The law has a public registry, bars an "operator" (מפעיל) from using unlicensed workers, and is enforced by administrative fines of ₪10k–40k rather than criminal law. The Ministry says existing workers have up to 18 months to get licensed before enforcement against unlicensed work, which by inference means roughly January 2027. Before the law, the Ministry's position was that the electrical side of AC work needed a חשמלאי-שירות licence in the cooling and AC field.

### Cited Findings
- **In force since 15.07.2025.** Only people trained and licensed by מינהל אסדרת עיסוקים may install or maintain cooling/AC systems. There are three licence types and transition provisions for current workers — [gov.il press release 15.07.2025](https://www.gov.il/he/pages/ac-law)
- **Grades:** דרגה 1 up to 18 kW cooling output, דרגה 2 up to 70 kW, דרגה 3 any system — [Nevo 232593](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law00/232593.htm); [Wikisource text](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/%D7%97%D7%95%D7%A7_%D7%94%D7%A1%D7%93%D7%A8%D7%AA_%D7%94%D7%A2%D7%99%D7%A1%D7%95%D7%A7_%D7%91%D7%A2%D7%91%D7%95%D7%93%D7%94_%D7%91%D7%9E%D7%A2%D7%A8%D7%9B%D7%AA_%D7%A7%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%A8_%D7%90%D7%95_%D7%9E%D7%99%D7%96%D7%95%D7%92_%D7%90%D7%95%D7%95%D7%99%D7%A8). The law was passed around 8 January 2025 (ח' בטבת תשפ"ה) and published in ס"ח תשפ"ה p. 182, per the Wikisource page.
- **§3(א):** no one may do cooling/AC work without a licence. **§18(א):** "מפעיל לא יפעיל אדם בביצוע עבודה במערכת קירור או מיזוג אוויר, אלא אם כן יש בידי אותו אדם רישיון" (an operator shall not use a person for cooling/AC work unless that person holds a licence). **§11(ב):** "המרשם יהיה פתוח לעיון הציבור בדרך של שאילתה שבה יצוינו פרטים מזהים" (the register is open to the public by a query giving identifying details) — [Wikisource](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/%D7%97%D7%95%D7%A7_%D7%94%D7%A1%D7%93%D7%A8%D7%AA_%D7%94%D7%A2%D7%99%D7%A1%D7%95%D7%A7_%D7%91%D7%A2%D7%91%D7%95%D7%93%D7%94_%D7%91%D7%9E%D7%A2%D7%A8%D7%9B%D7%AA_%D7%A7%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%A8_%D7%90%D7%95_%D7%9E%D7%99%D7%96%D7%95%D7%92_%D7%90%D7%95%D7%95%D7%99%D7%A8)
- **Scope of regulated work (First Schedule), summarised:** refrigerant handling, piping connection, electrical work within the system's own electrical parts, and testing. **§58:** a licence holder is treated as a חשמלאי-שירות for electrical work on cooling systems under the Electricity Law. **Fines (Third Schedule), summarised:** working with no licence ₪20,000 (individual); wrong licence grade ₪10,000; misleading advertising ₪5,000 individual / ₪10,000 corporation; operator/employer breaches ₪20,000–40,000 for a corporation. Validity is 5 years (§8). Commencement (§63): systems with flammable refrigerants first, others later; the enforcement chapter starts 18 months after commencement or when enforcement regulations take effect — [Nevo 232593](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law00/232593.htm). These amounts came through a summariser; verify against the Third Schedule text before relying on them.
- **The Ministry's own service page:**
  - Enforcement is administrative only: monetary sanctions, or licence suspension or cancellation by the registrar. The law "אינו קובע הפרות פליליות" (sets no criminal offences) and, for the first time for the Ministry, reaches people working with no licence at all.
  - "החוק מאפשר התארגנות של עד 18 חודשים לעוסקים בתחום. לאחר מכן תתבצע אכיפה עבור התחזות, או על עבודה ללא רישיון" (the law allows up to 18 months to get organised; after that, impersonation and unlicensed work will be enforced).
  - Licences last 5 years; renewal needs a professional refresher course and a fee. The fee is ₪288 (₪403 for a foreign expert). Licences are digital only, shown on a phone or printed.
  - Eligibility: 18+, Israeli citizen or resident, no disqualifying criminal record, and approved training (a Ministry of Labor supervised course, a Ministry of Education technology certificate, or registration as a practical engineer or technician in cooling).
  - Unit: RishuyAc@labor.gov.il, 073-2088482. Page updated 28.05.2026.
  - Source: [gov.il בקשה לרישוי מורשים במערכות קירור ומיזוג אוויר](https://www.gov.il/he/service/application-license-work-refrigeration-air-conditioning-system)
- **Before the law:** the Ministry's safety institute answered that an AC technician needs a suitable electrician licence for the electrical side, namely a חשמלאי-שירות in "מערכות קירור ומיזוג אוויר" (cooling and AC systems), under reg. 29 (answer dated 23.04.2018) — [osh.org.il](https://www.osh.org.il/heb/questionlobby/weekanswer,465/). The government training certificate for installing and servicing cooling/AC at grade 2 includes a service-electrician licence for cooling at level B — [search summary of college pages; secondary](https://ce.tcb.ac.il/courses/%D7%94%D7%AA%D7%A7%D7%A0%D7%94-%D7%95%D7%A9%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%9C%D7%9E%D7%AA%D7%A7%D7%A0%D7%99-%D7%A7%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%A8-%D7%95%D7%9E%D7%99%D7%96%D7%95%D7%92-%D7%90%D7%95%D7%95%D7%99/)
- **Motivation:** the law stems from Israel's international climate and refrigerant commitments. About 5,000 technicians worked in the residential market without formal training at the time (Dec 2019 report on the draft bill) — [ynet 09.12.2019](https://www.ynet.co.il/articles/0,7340,L-5638671,00.html)

### How to verify (AC)
- The law requires a public query-style register (§11). I found **no live public lookup URL** yet. The Ministry pages link only to a *self-service* "status check, reissue or licence copy" function for applicants, and the topic page lists no registry link — [topic page](https://www.gov.il/he/departments/topics/cooling-conditioning-systems). For now, collect the digital licence PDF (licence number, grade, expiry) and re-check once a registry appears.
- Manufacturer or importer authorisations (e.g. from the importers of Tadiran or Electra) are customary for warranty work but are not a legal requirement. I found no source listing them.

### Inferences
- From mid-2025 an AC installer or maintainer should hold an AC licence of grade ≥1. Enforcement against unlicensed work starts about 18 months after 15.07.2025, so roughly mid-January 2027. That date is an inference from the Ministry's "up to 18 months" wording plus the commencement date; the exact date was not confirmed.
- **Platform risk:** §18 bans an "operator" (מפעיל) from using unlicensed workers, with corporate fines up to about ₪40,000. Whether a dispatch marketplace is an "operator" depends on the statutory definition, which I did not extract. Treat it as a real risk and gate AC jobs on the licence.
- Filter cleaning is not exempted in the law. Even "service/cleaning" AC jobs should be treated as licensed work.

### Gaps
- The exact statutory definition of "מפעיל" and the exact commencement date per refrigerant class.
- The existence and URL of the §11 public register.
- Whether the old חשמלאי-שירות (cooling) licences were converted into the new AC licence.

## Q6. Solar water heaters (דוד שמש)

### Takeaway
No specific "solar-heater installer" licence was found. Two legal layers apply. (a) The product: ת"י 579 (the solar water-heater standard) is an official standard, so collectors and tanks must comply to be imported, made or sold. (b) The electrical side: per the Ministry of Labor, any heater work touching the electric element, thermostat, supply, earthing or meter needs at least a **חשמלאי-מעשי** licence; חשמלאי-שירות and מסויג do not qualify. Plumbing, roof and mechanical work is unlicensed.

### Cited Findings
- **Electrical side:** "עבודות הקשורות בהתקנה או בתחזוקה של דוודים וכן ביצוע אחת מפעולות החשמל הבאות… מחייבות ברישיון חשמל בתוקף מסוג חשמלאי - מעשי ומעלה (למעט חשמלאי שירות או חשמלאי מסויג)" (work installing or maintaining heaters that includes any of the listed electrical actions needs a valid licence of חשמלאי-מעשי or above, excluding service or restricted electricians) — [gov.il, Ministry of Labor, updated 16.06.2026](https://www.gov.il/he/pages/boiler-installation-011221)
- **Product standards:** ת"י 579.2 (storage tanks) and ת"י 579.1 (flat-plate collectors) are official standards: "חובה על פי חוק לייבא, לייצר ולשווק" (by law, only compliant products may be imported, made or sold). Local manufacturers must be under SII supervision and mark collectors with the standards mark. Electrical maintenance needs a licensed electrician — [מכון התקנים (the Standards Institution)](https://www.sii.org.il/he/electricalproducts)
- A claim that ת"י 579 requires replacing the whole system (piping, valves, electrics) when replacing a heater comes from an industry site only; not verified in primary text — [pro.co.il search summary](https://www.pro.co.il/boiler-technicians/guide/standard-note-for-solar-boilers)

### Inferences
- Platform gate for "boiler/element/thermostat" jobs: electrician licence ≥ מעשי. For pure solar-panel cleaning, piping or valve work, no licence is legally required.

### Gaps
- Whether ת"י 579 contains *installation* clauses binding the installer, and the planning-and-building sanitary regulations on solar heaters in buildings, were not verified from primary text.

## Q7. Refrigerator and washing-machine repair technicians

### Takeaway
No dedicated appliance-repair licence exists. However, household appliances are an explicit field of **חשמלאי-שירות** under reg. 29, and repairing an appliance's electrical parts plausibly counts as "electrical work" (repairing an installation that consumes electricity). The strict legal reading is that electrical repairs need a חשמלאי-שירות (household appliances) licence or higher. This is my inference, and enforcement practice was not found. Refrigerators also involve refrigerant: the 2025 AC law covers "cooling systems", but whether household fridges fall within it is unverified. "Authorised service lab" (מעבדה מורשית) status is a manufacturer or importer designation, not a legal licence.

### Cited Findings
- Reg. 29 service-electrician fields include "household electrical appliances (washing machines, refrigerators, vacuum cleaners, mixers, dishwashers, irons, dryers, heaters, ovens)" and "cooling and air-conditioning systems" — [Nevo 159_015](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law01/159_015.htm)
- Electrical work includes repair; an electrical installation includes installations used to consume electricity — [Electricity Law (Wikisource)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/%D7%97%D7%95%D7%A7_%D7%94%D7%97%D7%A9%D7%9E%D7%9C)
- The electrician lookup shows licence type and scope text, so a חשמלאי-שירות field can be verified — [data.labor.gov.il](https://data.labor.gov.il/Electricians/GetLicenseList.aspx)
- Appliance repair is not an item in the business-licensing order (checked through a summariser) — [צו רישוי עסקים (Wikisource)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/%D7%A6%D7%95_%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%A9%D7%95%D7%99_%D7%A2%D7%A1%D7%A7%D7%99%D7%9D_(%D7%A2%D7%A1%D7%A7%D7%99%D7%9D_%D7%98%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%A0%D7%99_%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%A9%D7%95%D7%99))

### Inferences
- A conservative platform policy for fridge and washer repair is to require an electrician licence: a חשמלאי-שירות in household appliances, or ≥ מעשי. The legally-minimum policy is to treat it as customary. This is a human decision (CLAUDE.md §4) and should be recorded as TBD.

### Gaps
- No primary guidance found on whether mechanical-only appliance repair (pumps, belts, door seals) is "electrical work".
- Whether the 2025 AC law's "cooling system" definition covers domestic refrigerators.
- Consumer-protection rules on importer service networks and authorised labs were not researched.

## Q8. Locksmith (door opening, cylinders, car lockout)

### Takeaway
I found **no statutory locksmith licence** in Israel, and locksmithing is not an item in the business-licensing order. Secondary sources conflict. Some claim a "police-approved locksmith" exists or that the law requires certification; another states explicitly that Israel Police does not regulate or license locksmiths, and that the only police document involved is a criminal-record certificate (תעודת יושר). The customary safeguard is checking the customer's ID and proof of residence before opening a door. I found no primary law mandating it.

### Cited Findings
- The business-licensing order has no item for locks, locksmiths or lock-breaking (summariser check) — [צו רישוי עסקים (Wikisource)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/%D7%A6%D7%95_%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%A9%D7%95%D7%99_%D7%A2%D7%A1%D7%A7%D7%99%D7%9D_(%D7%A2%D7%A1%D7%A7%D7%99%D7%9D_%D7%98%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%A0%D7%99_%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%A9%D7%95%D7%99))
- **Conflict between secondary sources:**
  - A law-firm article claims "the law in Israel requires every locksmith to undergo approval that includes specific certification for lock-breaking", followed by a police certificate and then a business licence. It cites no statute — [nir-law](https://www.nir-law.co.il/%D7%94%D7%90%D7%9D-%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%AA%D7%A8-%D7%9C%D7%91%D7%A7%D7%A9-%D7%9E%D7%9E%D7%A0%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%9C%D7%9F-%D7%9E%D7%A1%D7%9E%D7%9A-%D7%A2%D7%9C-%D7%94%D7%99%D7%A2%D7%93%D7%A8-%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%A9)
  - Contradicted by a locksmith training centre: Israel Police is not a regulator and does not license locksmiths; it issues only a certificate of no criminal record — [טאגור סנטר](https://www.tagorc.co.il/%D7%9E%D7%A0%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%9C%D7%9F-%D7%90%D7%99%D7%A9%D7%95%D7%A8-%D7%9E%D7%A9%D7%98%D7%A8%D7%94-%D7%9C%D7%9E%D7%94-%D7%96%D7%94-%D7%97%D7%A9%D7%95%D7%91-%D7%94%D7%90%D7%9D-%D7%96%D7%94-%D7%97/)
  - Marketing sites advertising "מנעולן באישור משטרה" (police-approved locksmith) are commercial claims, not evidence — [e.g. orlock](https://orlock.co.il/24-hour-locksmith/israel-police-licensed-locksmith/)

### Inferences
- There is no registry to verify against. The platform's controls are product decisions: identity verification of the professional, and possibly a criminal-record certificate. For door or car openings, a mandatory in-app step where the professional confirms the customer's ID matches the address (or vehicle registration) before opening, with a logged photo or record.
- **Caution on criminal records:** חוק המידע הפלילי ותקנת השבים (the criminal-records and rehabilitation law) restricts who may demand a person's criminal record, and generally forbids demanding a self-obtained copy. Requiring a תעודת יושר from locksmiths may itself be legally problematic. This comes from my background knowledge of the law, not from a source read here, and needs legal review.

### Gaps
- No primary source (police procedure, statute) on locksmith door-opening protocols or proof-of-residence requirements.
- No primary text on the criminal-records-law restriction as applied to platforms.

## Q9. Platform-level risk: penalties for a marketplace dispatching unlicensed workers

### Takeaway
The clearest explicit exposure is the **2025 AC law** (§18 operator ban, administrative fines up to about ₪40,000 for a corporation). The Electricity Law and LPG regulations, as summarised, impose offences on the *performer*: up to one year's imprisonment under Electricity Law §11. I found no explicit "orderer or intermediary" offence in them. The Ministry of Labor publicly instructs anyone employing an electrician to check validity online and not rely on the paper licence. That creates a strong reasonable-care expectation.

### Cited Findings
- AC law §18(א): an operator may not use an unlicensed person. Operator/employer fines are ₪20,000–40,000 for a corporation. Enforcement against unlicensed work follows an 18-month grace period — [Wikisource](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/%D7%97%D7%95%D7%A7_%D7%94%D7%A1%D7%93%D7%A8%D7%AA_%D7%94%D7%A2%D7%99%D7%A1%D7%95%D7%A7_%D7%91%D7%A2%D7%91%D7%95%D7%93%D7%94_%D7%91%D7%9E%D7%A2%D7%A8%D7%9B%D7%AA_%D7%A7%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%A8_%D7%90%D7%95_%D7%9E%D7%99%D7%96%D7%95%D7%92_%D7%90%D7%95%D7%95%D7%99%D7%A8); [Nevo](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law00/232593.htm); [gov.il service page](https://www.gov.il/he/service/application-license-work-refrigeration-air-conditioning-system)
- Electricity Law §11: one year's imprisonment and/or a fine for breaching the law. No separate employer or orderer offence was found in the summarised text — [Nevo](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law01/999_468.htm)
- Ministry of Labor: "בכל מקרה של העסקת בעל רישיון חשמל, ייבדק תוקף רישיונו באתר מינהל אסדרת עיסוקים, בנוסף להצגת רישיון מקורי" (whenever an electrician is employed, check licence validity on the regulator's site as well as seeing the original licence) — [gov.il](https://www.gov.il/he/pages/limited-license)
- LPG: work without a licence "is an offence"; the worker must show the licence to the customer on request — [gov.il](https://www.gov.il/he/pages/lpg_license); [Nevo 999_620](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law01/999_620.htm)

### Inferences
- Beyond statutory fines, the realistic risks are:
  - civil negligence and vicarious-liability claims after a fire, gas leak or electric shock caused by an unlicensed worker the platform dispatched;
  - insurers refusing cover;
  - consumer-protection misrepresentation claims if the app presents workers as "verified" or "licensed" when they are not.
  - None of these was researched against primary sources here.
- Possible criminal aiding liability (חוק העונשין §31, aiding an offence) for knowingly dispatching an unlicensed electrician or gas worker is conceivable but unverified.
- **Recommended verification model, per service:**
  - Electrician, water-heater element, alarm with mains connection: Ministry of Labor lookup, checking type and scope text plus expiry. Re-check after each 30 April.
  - Gas: Ministry of Energy lookup, type level 1 or 2, plus expiry.
  - AC: the 2025 licence at grade ≥1. Collect the digital licence now; the public register is not yet found.
  - Plumbing and locksmith: no legal licence exists. Rely on identity, business registration and insurance. The customer-identity check for lockouts is a product policy.
  - Both government lookups are free and captcha-free, but neither has a published API or reuse terms. Automated scraping is a legal and terms-of-use question for the owner (CLAUDE.md §4-style decision); a manual or assisted check by admin is the safe default.

### Gaps
- The exact definition of "מפעיל" in the AC law, and whether it would cover a marketplace.
- Case law on platform liability for dispatching unlicensed tradespeople in Israel.
- Current shekel amounts of the Electricity Law and LPG-law fines.
