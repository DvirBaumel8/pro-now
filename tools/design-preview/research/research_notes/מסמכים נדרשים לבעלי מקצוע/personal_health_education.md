# Israel (2026): legal vs customary requirements for home-visit personal, health, education and cleaning professionals, and how a platform can verify them

Research date: 2026-09-29. Scope: Israel only. Many gov.il pages returned HTTP 403 to the fetch tool, so some official pages are cited from search-result listings and were not read in full. Those citations are marked "(listing only)". Treat them as pointers to check, not as verified text.

## Q1. Per trade: what is legally required, what is customary, who is the authority, and how a platform can verify it

### Takeaway
Only the **home-visit doctor** has a hard personal licence with a free public registry that a platform can query automatically. It is the Ministry of Health (MoH) licence, and the registry also shows specialist status. **Sports coaching** requires a certificate only for some sports and levels: fitness training of adult amateurs is generally exempt. **Hair, nails and cosmetics** are licensed by the **premises** (business licence, item 1.4 of the licensing order), not by a personal licence. **Massage, makeup, tutoring and cleaning** have no personal occupational licence. Cleaning **companies** that supply staff need a Ministry of Labour contractor licence.

### Cited findings

**Home-visit doctor (רופא)**
- MoH is the only body the law empowers to license the practice of medicine in Israel. The Health Professions Regulation Law of 2008 (חוק הסדרת העיסוק במקצועות הבריאות) regulates many other health professions — [ask-lawyer summary](https://www.ask-lawyer.co.il/pages/%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%A9%D7%95%D7%99-%D7%9E%D7%A7%D7%A6%D7%95%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%94%D7%91%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%90%D7%95%D7%AA) (secondary source); law text at [Wikisource](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/%D7%97%D7%95%D7%A7_%D7%94%D7%A1%D7%93%D7%A8%D7%AA_%D7%94%D7%A2%D7%99%D7%A1%D7%95%D7%A7_%D7%91%D7%9E%D7%A7%D7%A6%D7%95%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%AA_%D7%94%D7%91%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%90%D7%95%D7%AA).
- **Public web lookup:** the MoH practitioners registries at https://registries.health.gov.il/Practitioners and the physicians' register (פנקס הרופאים) at https://practitioners.health.gov.il/Practitioners/1. You can search by name, licence number or specialty. According to the search-result summary, it shows specialty, licence type (permanent or temporary), licence number and licence **status** (active, suspended, permanently suspended). The service page is at [gov.il service](https://www.gov.il/he/service/licensed-medical-practitioners) (listing only; returned 403 to fetch). Wikipedia notes that the register shows the licence number, specialist number, city and licence date — [Wikipedia: פנקס הרופאים](https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D7%A4%D7%A0%A7%D7%A1_%D7%94%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%A4%D7%90%D7%99%D7%9D).
- **Automatable open dataset (verified live on 2026-09-29):** the data.gov.il dataset "מאגר רישיונות רופאים משרד הבריאות", resource `9c64c522-bbc2-48fe-96fb-3b2a8626f59e`, answers on the public CKAN API `https://data.gov.il/api/3/action/datastore_search?resource_id=9c64c522-bbc2-48fe-96fb-3b2a8626f59e`. No key is needed. It held **64,712 records**. The fields are: first name, last name, doctor licence number, licence registration date, specialist-certificate number, specialist registration date and specialty name. There is one row per specialty. **The dataset has no licence-status field**, so suspension or revocation is not visible there. Status must come from the registries.health.gov.il web lookup — [data.gov.il dataset page](https://data.gov.il/he/datasets/ministry-health/database-of-doctors-licenses-moh/9c64c522-bbc2-48fe-96fb-3b2a8626f59e) plus a direct API call made during this research.
- **Malpractice insurance:** there is no universal statutory duty. Government-arranged cover applies to hospital staff. Doctors in private practice, and even doctors working in the health funds (HMOs), must insure themselves. State committees recommended making it mandatory, but that has not been enacted as far as found — [IMA page on professional liability insurance](https://m.ima.org.il/MainSite/ViewCategory.aspx?CategoryId=7855); [MedReviews](https://www.medreviews.co.il/article/medical-liability-insurance-practical-highlights); related Supreme Court case on a hospital that did not insure its doctors, [Globes](https://www.globes.co.il/news/article.aspx?did=1001143502).
- Platform verification: (a) match the ID or licence number against the data.gov.il API, which is free and automatable, to confirm the licence and specialty; (b) check status on registries.health.gov.il, which is a web form with no documented API, so scraping or a manual check is needed; (c) collect the insurance policy document, which cannot be checked against any public registry.

**Hairdresser (ספר/מעצב שיער) and manicure/pedicure (מניקור/פדיקור)**
- The Business Licensing Order lists item **1.4 "non-medical treatments on the human body"**. Item 1.4a covers "beauty and cosmetics treatment, pedicure and manicure, tanning salon" and item 1.4b covers "hair salon". MoH's role is listed as "for information only". Fire-service approval depends on floor area. These items follow a shortened licensing track with a licence valid for 10 years — [buslic 1.4a FAQ](https://www.buslic.co.il/faq/1-4a/) (secondary source); official uniform specification: [gov.il uniform spec 1.4a](https://www.gov.il/he/pages/uniform-spec-01-04a) (listing only; returned 403).
- The licensing framework covers a **place of business**. The pages reviewed say nothing about mobile or home-visit service — [buslic 1.4a](https://www.buslic.co.il/faq/1-4a/).
- Tel Aviv municipality publishes an owner's self-inspection checklist for hair salons, beauty, manicure/pedicure and tanning businesses — [Tel Aviv checklist PDF](https://www.tel-aviv.gov.il/Business/BusinessLicense/DocLib/%D7%A8%D7%A9%D7%99%D7%9E%D7%AA%20%D7%AA%D7%99%D7%95%D7%92%20%D7%9C%D7%9E%D7%99%D7%9C%D7%95%D7%99%20%D7%91%D7%A2%D7%9C%D7%99%20%D7%A2%D7%A1%D7%A7%20%D7%9C%D7%A2%D7%A1%D7%A7%D7%99%20%D7%9E%D7%A1%D7%A4%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%AA,%20%D7%98%D7%99%D7%A4%D7%95%D7%9C%20%D7%99%D7%95%D7%A4%D7%99%20%D7%95%D7%A7%D7%95%D7%A1%D7%9E%D7%98%D7%99%D7%A7%D7%94,%20%D7%A4%D7%93%D7%99%D7%A7%D7%95%D7%A8%20%D7%9E%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%A7%D7%95%D7%A8,%20%D7%95%D7%9E%D7%9B%D7%95%D7%A0%D7%99%20%D7%A9%D7%99%D7%96%D7%95%D7%A3.pdf) (not opened).
- No personal occupational licence for hairdressers or nail technicians was found in any source reviewed. Diplomas from private schools are what is customary.

**Makeup artist (מאפרת)**
- No licensing source was found. Makeup is not named in item 1.4a in the sources reviewed ("beauty and cosmetics treatment" may arguably include it) — [buslic 1.4a](https://www.buslic.co.il/faq/1-4a/). Treat it as unregulated except for general business registration.

**Personal trainer (מאמן כושר)**
- The Sports Regulations (Certification Requirement), 5778-2018, reg. 2(a): a person may not coach a sport listed in the First Schedule, paid or unpaid, without the appropriate certificate. There are two tiers: "sports guide" (מדריך) and "sports coach" (מאמן). **Exemption:** coaching amateur and casual athletes **of all ages** in many Part A sports needs no certificate. Some sports are exempt only for amateurs over 18 (for example gymnastics and martial arts). Part B higher-risk sports (for example boxing, judo, climbing and riding) require a certificate for all coaching. The latest amendment found is No. 2, 6 May 2022 — [Wikisource: תקנות הספורט (חיוב בתעודת הסמכה)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/%D7%AA%D7%A7%D7%A0%D7%95%D7%AA_%D7%94%D7%A1%D7%A4%D7%95%D7%A8%D7%98_(%D7%97%D7%99%D7%95%D7%91_%D7%91%D7%AA%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%93%D7%AA_%D7%94%D7%A1%D7%9E%D7%9B%D7%94)).
- The Sports Law (חוק הספורט, 1988) is the parent law — [Wikisource: חוק הספורט](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/%D7%97%D7%95%D7%A7_%D7%94%D7%A1%D7%A4%D7%95%D7%A8%D7%98).
- Government bills to regulate sports coaching as an occupation were published in 2015 and 2021 — [2015 memorandum](https://www.tazkirim.gov.il/sfc/servlet.shepherd/version/download/0683Y000009E2zbQAC); [2021 memorandum](https://tazkirim.gov.il/s/legislativeworkactivity/a133Y00000K4O1pQAF/%D7%94%D7%A4%D7%A6%D7%94-%D7%9C%D7%94%D7%A2%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%A6%D7%99%D7%91%D7%95%D7%A8?language=iw). Whether either became law was not confirmed.
- The Fitness Institutes Law (חוק מכוני כושר, 1994) regulates **gyms** (premises), not freelance trainers at a client's home — [Nevo](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law01/p212m1_023.htm); [Wikipedia](https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D7%97%D7%95%D7%A7_%D7%9E%D7%9B%D7%95%D7%A0%D7%99_%D7%9B%D7%95%D7%A9%D7%A8). Separate regulations cover training minors in a gym — [Wikisource](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/%D7%AA%D7%A7%D7%A0%D7%95%D7%AA_%D7%9E%D7%9B%D7%95%D7%A0%D7%99_%D7%9B%D7%95%D7%A9%D7%A8_(%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%A9%D7%95%D7%99_%D7%95%D7%A4%D7%99%D7%A7%D7%95%D7%97)_(%D7%90%D7%99%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%9F_%D7%A7%D7%98%D7%99%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%9D_%D7%91%D7%9E%D7%9B%D7%95%D7%9F_%D7%9B%D7%95%D7%A9%D7%A8)).
- Certificates are issued by recognised coaching schools, and there is a procedure for recognising foreign diplomas — [sport-ins (broker article)](https://sport-ins.com/sport-and-insurance-articles/overseas-fitness-diploma/) (secondary source).

**Massage therapist (מעסה) / complementary medicine**
- No licence is needed to work as a masseur. Massage is recognised for health-insurance purposes only when a (licensed) physiotherapist provides it — search-result synthesis of [ask-lawyer](https://www.ask-lawyer.co.il/pages/%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%A9%D7%95%D7%99-%D7%9E%D7%A7%D7%A6%D7%95%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%94%D7%91%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%90%D7%95%D7%AA) and related pages (secondary source; the exact sentence was not verified on the page).
- The licensed professions listed by MoH include medicine, dentistry, pharmacy, optometry, hypnosis, speech therapy, dietetics, physiotherapy and others under the 2008 law. Massage, reflexology and other complementary-medicine practices are **not** among them — same source; [MoH licensing landing page](https://www.gov.il/he/departments/topics/medical-professions-licensing/govil-landing-page) (listing only).

**Private tutor (מורה פרטי)** — see Q3 for the sex-offender law. There is no teaching licence for private tutoring. The Ministry of Education licence applies to teachers in the formal school system. No source was found requiring a licence for private tutors.

**Home cleaning / post-renovation cleaning**
- There is no occupational licence for an individual cleaner (none found).
- **Cleaning companies:** under the Employment of Workers by Manpower Contractors Law, 1996, a "service contractor" (קבלן שירות) is anyone whose business is providing a service listed in the Second Schedule (guarding, security, **cleaning**) **through its employees at another person's premises**. Such contractors need a Ministry of Labour licence. The threshold conditions include having no criminal convictions, and a bank guarantee is required — [Nevo: the 1996 law](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law01/116_001.htm); [gov.il: application for a manpower or service contractor licence (guarding, security, cleaning)](https://www.gov.il/he/service/manpower-contractor-license-request) (listing only); [Kol Zchut: manpower contractor workers](https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%91%D7%93%D7%99_%D7%A7%D7%91%D7%9C%D7%9F_%D7%9B%D7%95%D7%97_%D7%90%D7%93%D7%9D).
- A separate 2013 law governs cleaning and guarding contractors that serve **public bodies** — [Nevo](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law00/127620.htm). It is not relevant to private homes.

### Inferences
- A **self-employed** cleaner working alone has no employees, so the service-contractor licence should not apply. A platform that **onboards cleaning companies** that send their employees would expect them to hold the Ministry of Labour licence. It is unclear whether the platform itself could be treated as a contractor or manpower contractor if it effectively employs or directs cleaners. That is a worker-classification risk a lawyer should assess.
- For hair, nails and cosmetics, the business licence is tied to premises. A purely mobile professional who works only in clients' homes probably has no premises to license. This is **unconfirmed**: no source addressed mobile service directly.
- Fitness at home for adult amateurs is almost certainly **not** legally gated by a certificate under the 2018 regulations. A Wingate or recognised-school certificate is customary and is what insurers ask for.

### Gaps
- MoH hygiene guidance for hairdressers, cosmetics, manicure and pedicure (sterilisation, autoclave, single-use tools) could not be retrieved: gov.il returned 403 and search found only COVID-era guidance. Whether any MoH rule applies to mobile or home-visit nail or beauty work was not established.
- No public registry of sports-coaching certificates was found (the Ministry of Culture and Sport or Wingate). The 2021 coaching-regulation bill's status was not confirmed.
- The exact text of the First Schedule (whether "כושר גופני" / fitness is listed, and in which part) was not quoted verbatim. The fetch tool's summary was used.
- No source explicitly confirms that makeup artists fall inside or outside item 1.4a.

## Q2. Which trades are unregulated (only business registration needed)?

### Takeaway
Based on the sources found: makeup artist, massage therapist, private tutor, individual home cleaner and personal trainer (for adult amateurs) have **no personal occupational licence**. Hair and nail work needs a business licence only for the premises. Only the doctor has a mandatory personal licence.

### Cited findings
- Massage: no licence needed — [ask-lawyer](https://www.ask-lawyer.co.il/pages/%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%A9%D7%95%D7%99-%D7%9E%D7%A7%D7%A6%D7%95%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%94%D7%91%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%90%D7%95%D7%AA) (secondary source).
- Trainer: adult amateur coaching is exempt in many sports — [Wikisource regs](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/%D7%AA%D7%A7%D7%A0%D7%95%D7%AA_%D7%94%D7%A1%D7%A4%D7%95%D7%A8%D7%98_(%D7%97%D7%99%D7%95%D7%91_%D7%91%D7%AA%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%93%D7%AA_%D7%94%D7%A1%D7%9E%D7%9B%D7%94)).
- Hair, nails and cosmetics: item 1.4 is a premises licence — [buslic](https://www.buslic.co.il/faq/1-4a/).
- Doctor: MoH licence required — [MoH registry](https://registries.health.gov.il/Practitioners).

### Inferences
- "Business registration" here means registering with the Tax Authority as an exempt or licensed dealer (עוסק פטור/מורשה) and with National Insurance. That applies to all self-employed people. It was not researched in this note because it belongs to another researcher's scope, so no citation is given.

### Gaps
- Tutors and makeup artists are "unregulated" only because no regulating law was found (absence of evidence).

## Q3. Criminal-record checks: which laws require or allow a certificate, how a person gets it, and whether a platform may ask for a תעודת יושר

### Takeaway
**As a rule, a platform may NOT demand a criminal-record extract (תעודת יושר), even indirectly by asking the candidate to bring one.** Section 22 of the Criminal Register and Rehabilitation of Offenders Law makes that a criminal offence punishable by up to 2 years' imprisonment. The main lawful exception is the **police approval under the Law for the Prevention of Employment of Sex Offenders in Certain Institutions (2001)**. That law applies to work in a defined "institution" (מוסד) serving minors or people with disabilities. Whether it reaches a private tutor hired directly or through a platform is **unclear, and likely not**: a child's home is not a listed institution.

### Cited findings
- Criminal Register law s.22(b): anyone who obtains or demands register information they are not entitled to, directly or indirectly, for employment or for a decision about the person, faces **2 years' imprisonment**. The legislature expressly targeted employers who ask the candidate to bring the extract themselves — [WorkRights](https://www.workrights.co.il/%D7%AA%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%93%D7%AA-%D7%99%D7%95%D7%A9%D7%A8); [Mako](https://www.mako.co.il/study-career-career/articles/Article-ac327096305a771026.htm); [Calcalist](https://www.calcalist.co.il/local/articles/0,7340,L-3890834,00.html) (secondary sources; primary text on Nevo not fetched).
- Sex-offender law, section 1: "מוסד" (institution) covers schools and educational institutions where minors stay, residential and day-care centres and home day-care for minors (משפחתונים), kindergartens, zoos, playgrounds, **youth movements**, **businesses that transport minors**, day camps, boarding schools and clubs where minors stay. It also covers residential, day and rehabilitation-work centres for people with disabilities, certain businesses providing services to people, and hospitals, clinics and nursing wards — [Wikisource: the law](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/%D7%97%D7%95%D7%A7_%D7%9C%D7%9E%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%A2%D7%AA_%D7%94%D7%A2%D7%A1%D7%A7%D7%94_%D7%A9%D7%9C_%D7%A2%D7%91%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%99%D7%A0%D7%99_%D7%9E%D7%99%D7%9F_%D7%91%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%A1%D7%93%D7%95%D7%AA_%D7%9E%D7%A1%D7%95%D7%99%D7%9E%D7%99%D7%9D) (read through a summarising fetch tool; the list may be incomplete).
- "עבודה" (work) is defined as work paid or voluntary, **including providing services**, that gives regular or ongoing contact with minors (or the other protected groups). Section 2 says an employer may not hire or employ in an institution an adult convicted of a sex offence. Section 5 says that breaching s.2(b), which binds the offender himself, carries **one year's imprisonment**. The latest amendment seen is from 5784 (2024) — [Wikisource](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/%D7%97%D7%95%D7%A7_%D7%9C%D7%9E%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%A2%D7%AA_%D7%94%D7%A2%D7%A1%D7%A7%D7%94_%D7%A9%D7%9C_%D7%A2%D7%91%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%99%D7%A0%D7%99_%D7%9E%D7%99%D7%9F_%D7%91%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%A1%D7%93%D7%95%D7%AA_%D7%9E%D7%A1%D7%95%D7%99%D7%9E%D7%99%D7%9D).
- An employer must not take on an adult before receiving police confirmation that nothing under the law bars the employment — [Synerion legal blog](https://www.synerionhcm.co.il/2021/01/%D7%97%D7%95%D7%A7-%D7%9C%D7%9E%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%A2%D7%AA-%D7%94%D7%A2%D7%A1%D7%A7%D7%AA-%D7%A2%D7%91%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%99%D7%A0%D7%99-%D7%9E%D7%99%D7%9F/). The regulations on the police approval (2003) — [Nevo](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law01/999_150.htm). The Ministry of Education procedure — [MoE circular](https://meyda.education.gov.il/files/hozermankal/valid/k-2013-2-1-5-3-68.pdf).
- **How to obtain it:** the online police service "בקשה לקבלת אישור העסקה מהמשטרה" at https://www.gov.il/he/service/application-for-police-approval. It covers employment in institutions where employing **sex offenders and violent offenders** is prohibited. The applicant fills in an online form and completes identity verification (listing only; the page returned 403). An older version of the service: https://www.gov.il/he/service/application-for-police-approval-old. Guides for the adult applicant's form ("טופס בגיר"): [lavy.org.il PDF](https://lavy.org.il/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/%D7%9E%D7%93%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%9A-%D7%9C%D7%94%D7%95%D7%A6%D7%90%D7%AA-%D7%91%D7%92%D7%99%D7%A8-%D7%9E%D7%A7%D7%95%D7%95%D7%9F.pdf); [Yedidim PDF](https://www.yedidim.org.il/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/%D7%94%D7%A1%D7%91%D7%A8-%D7%9C%D7%94%D7%95%D7%A6%D7%90%D7%AA-%D7%90%D7%99%D7%A9%D7%95%D7%A8-%D7%94%D7%A2%D7%93%D7%A8-%D7%A2%D7%91%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%A0%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%9E%D7%99%D7%9F.pdf); [tofes101](https://tofes101.co.il/forms/police-adult-certificate/).
- **The approval is not proof of innocence:** there was a publicised case where the police issued an approval to a teacher then under indictment for sex offences. Pending charges may not appear — [Ynet](https://www.ynet.co.il/articles/0,7340,L-4972214,00.html); [Israel Hayom](https://www.israelhayom.co.il/article/481919). One reported conviction involved a teacher who abused a pupil during **private maths lessons** — [Ynet](https://www.ynet.co.il/articles/0,7340,L-4972214,00.html).

### Inferences
- A platform (or a family) hiring a **self-employed tutor for lessons in the child's home** probably does not fall under "מוסד", so the sex-offender approval probably cannot be demanded as a legal requirement there. At the same time, s.22 of the Criminal Register law bars demanding a general criminal-record extract. **Net effect:** a platform most likely cannot lawfully require a criminal-record extract from tutors, cleaners, beauticians and similar professionals. It may be able to require the sex-offender police approval **only** if it or the service falls inside a listed "institution" category (for example a business whose main activity is services to minors, or services to people with disabilities). **This needs an Israeli lawyer's opinion before launch.** It also matches CLAUDE.md §4 ("background-check policy" is a human decision).
- The approval is issued per employer or institution. A platform-level "badge" would need a legal basis and the police's own process, not a copy of a document uploaded by the professional.
- There is no public registry, so none of the criminal-record checks can be automated.

### Gaps
- The verbatim full list of "מוסד" paragraphs was not obtained (Kol Zchut and gov.il returned 403). It is therefore unconfirmed whether a paragraph covers "a business whose occupations include services to minors" (for example private lessons or clubs). That would decide whether a tutoring platform is covered.
- No source was found on whether the Criminal Register law's schedules give any body the right to get record information for people who **enter homes** (cleaners, home carers of elderly people). Not researched: tool-call budget reached.
- The police approval's cost, turnaround and validity period, and whether it is delivered to the applicant or to the employer in the current online flow, were not confirmed (gov.il returned 403).
